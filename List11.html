🔷 长难句拆解｜whose 定语从句、宾语从句与 as though 虚拟语气嵌套的并列长句

句子骨架：I remember the morning (whose largest headline stared up…); its phrasing seemed to imply that…, as though…had been ordered to…
I still remember the morning whose largest headline stared up from the breakfast page; its phrasing seemed to imply that the country had chosen to leave its old self behind, as though a recurring nightmare had been ordered to pass and never return.
1主语I主句主语，以第一人称总领全句，奠定回忆的叙述基调。
2谓语still remember一般现在时谓语，still 强调记忆延续至今，与宾语 the morning 构成动宾核心。
3定语从句whose largest headline stared up from the breakfast pagewhose 引导限制性定语从句修饰 the morning，whose 在从句中作 headline 的定语，相当于 the morning's largest headline；stare up 为拟人用法。
4宾语从句that the country had chosen to leave its old self behind作 imply 的宾语；had chosen 为过去完成时，表“选择”这一动作先于 seemed；choose to do sth 后接不定式短语 leave…behind。
5方式状语从句as though a recurring nightmare had been ordered to pass and never returnas though 引导方式状语从句，谓语用 had been ordered 这一过去完成时的被动式表示假想；to pass 与 never return 为并列宾补。
  • 分号连接两个并列分句，第二句的 its phrasing 回指第一句中的 headline，阅读时可在分号处先断开，再分别找主谓。
  • 关系代词 whose 在定语从句中只能作定语，表示所属关系，本句中即“那个早晨的最大标题”。
  • as though/if 引导的从句可用虚拟语气：对过去情况的假想用 had done；本句还叠加了被动语态 had been done。
  • 句中嵌套 seem to do、choose to do、order sth to do 三组不定式结构，形成层层后置的非谓语链条。

🔷 长难句拆解｜现在分词后置定语与冒号后四个并列名词性从句

句子骨架：A teacher (sitting on a wooden bench…) can catch the mood before anyone else: whether…, why…, which…and which…
A well-meaning teacher sitting on a wooden bench outside the school can still catch the mood before anyone else: whether the crop will be sold, why the river's fish decrease year by year, which families stand in favor with the local committee and which have fallen out of favour since the last election.
1主语A well-meaning teacher合成形容词 well-meaning（善意的）作前置定语，是全句的动作发出者。
2后置定语sitting on a wooden bench outside the school现在分词短语作后置定语修饰 teacher，可还原为定语从句 who is sitting…；on a bench 与 outside the school 层层作地点状语。
3谓语can still catchcatch the mood 为固定搭配，意为“捕捉到情绪/氛围”；before anyone else 为比较意味的时间状语。
4宾语从句（并列一、二）whether the crop will be sold, why the river's fish decrease year by year冒号后具体说明 the mood 的内容；whether 与 why 各引导一个名词性从句，是老师关切的前两个问题。
5宾语从句（并列三、四）which families stand in favor with the local committee and which have fallen out of favour since the last election两个 which 从句由 and 并列；stand in favor with 与 fall out of favour 反义对照，since 引导时间状语从句。
  • 冒号后的 whether/why/which/which 四个名词性从句并列，共同解释抽象名词 the mood 的具体内涵，是全句的信息重心。
  • 单个现在分词短语 sitting… 作后置定语时可还原为 who is sitting…，译为汉语时常提前为“……的老师”。
  • stand in favor with sb 与 fall out of favour 构成反义平行结构；favor/favour 为美式与英式拼写差异，文中有意并用。
  • year by year 为“介词by+同一名词”构成的名词短语作状语，表逐年渐变，同类还有 day by day、little by little。

🔷 长难句拆解｜“介词+whom”非限制性定语从句与 recommend 后的并列虚拟从句

句子骨架：Such claims annoy the planners…, for whom the boundary…continues to blur; a consulting firm (applying…) recommended that…receive…and that…ditch…
Such claims annoy the planners in the capital, for whom the boundary between city and countryside continues to blur; a consulting firm, applying strict quantitative criteria, recommended that every household receive a device capable of monitoring irrigation and that the village finally ditch its paper ledgers.
1主语Such claimsSuch 回指上一段中那位经理的说法，使段与段之间衔接紧密。
2非限制性定语从句for whom the boundary between city and countryside continues to blur“介词+关系代词”引导非限制性定语从句，for whom 相当于 for the planners；between city and countryside 作 boundary 的后置定语，continue to do 表“持续做”。
3插入状语applying strict quantitative criteria现在分词短语作插入性方式状语，逻辑主语是 a consulting firm，相当于 which applied…，前后用逗号与主干隔开。
4谓语recommended后接两个 that 引导的宾语从句；recommend 表“建议”时，从句谓语用“(should+)动词原形”的现在虚拟式。
5宾语从句（虚拟一）that every household receive a device capable of monitoring irrigationreceive 前省略了 should；形容词短语 capable of monitoring… 作后置定语修饰 device，相当于定语从句 that is capable of…。
6宾语从句（虚拟二）that the village finally ditch its paper ledgers与前一 that 从句并列共用 recommended；ditch 此处为动词“抛弃、丢弃”，同样是省略 should 的虚拟式。
  • “介词+whom/which”引导定语从句时，介词由先行词的搭配决定：the planners for whom → for whom，不可漏掉介词。
  • recommend、suggest、insist、demand、propose 等表建议或要求的动词后，that 从句用“(should+)动词原形”，本句两个从句均省去 should。
  • 两个 that 从句由 and 并列共用一个谓语动词 recommended，第二个 that 不宜省略，以保持从句层次清晰。
  • 现在分词 applying… 作方式状语可替换为定语从句 which applied…；capable of doing 作后置定语则相当于 that can do，是考研常见的非谓语简化手段。
