🔷 长难句拆解｜so ... that ... 结果状语从句 + 分号并列句 + 过去完成时

句子骨架：前一分句 They offered a mortgage deal so generous that ... + 分号后 a predecessor had made ... and then retreated + when 时间状语从句
They offered a mortgage deal so generous that it looked almost implausible; a predecessor of the present manager had made the same offer and then retreated when the price of coal fell.
1前一分句They offered a mortgage deal so generous that it looked almost implausibleso + 形容词 + that 引导结果状语从句，it 指 the deal
2分号后的并列句a predecessor of the present manager had made the same offer and then retreated过去完成时 had made 表示更早的动作，retreated 与其并列
3时间状语从句when the price of coal fellwhen 从句用一般过去时，交代退走的时机
  • 分号连接两个独立分句，前后是“今年条件更优厚”与“十年前同样条件却退走”的对比。
  • so generous that ... 中 that 引导结果状语从句，it 指代前面的 a mortgage deal。
  • 过去完成时 had made 与一般过去时 fell 形成时间层次，是时间线题的高频考点。

🔷 长难句拆解｜同位语 + so ... that ... 结果状语从句

句子骨架：主句 The company's advisers called my mother an amateur + 同位语 a claim + so ... that 结果从句
The company's external advisers called my mother an amateur, a claim so implausible that even the editor laughed.
1主句The company's external advisers called my mother an amateurcall sb sth 结构，an amateur 作宾语补足语
2同位语a claim so implausible that even the editor laugheda claim 复指前面整句；so ... that ... 引导结果状语从句
  • a claim 是同位语，把前面整句话压缩成一个名词短语，是议论文常见写法。
  • so + 形容词 + that 从句表示“如此……以至于”，that 在从句中不作成分。
  • even the editor laughed 用 even 加强语气，暗示前面的话荒谬到极点。

🔷 长难句拆解｜非限制性定语从句 + 宾语从句 + 比较结构

句子骨架：主语 The head teacher + 非限制性定语从句（who ...）+ said 后的宾语从句（mattered more than）
The head teacher, who had once been a counselor and still spelled the word counsellor on the old sign, said my engagement with the children mattered more than my degree.
1主语The head teacher全句主语，后面两个逗号之间只是补充说明
2非限制性定语从句who had once been a counselor and still spelled the word counsellor on the old signwho 指代 the head teacher，从句内 had been 与 spelled 并列
3主句谓语 + 宾语从句said my engagement with the children mattered more than my degreesaid 后省略 that，宾语从句主语是 my engagement，谓语是 mattered
  • 非限制性定语从句用逗号隔开，去掉后主句仍完整，只起补充信息作用。
  • 宾语从句中 mattered more than my degree 是比较结构，mattered 为不及物动词。
