🔷 长难句拆解｜however 引导的让步插入语与 because 原因状语从句

句子骨架：No single instrument ..., however elegant, could confine ..., because its ... component—...—remains ... and travels ...
No single instrument of policy, however elegant, could confine the technology within national borders, because its most important component—mathematical knowledge—remains relevant and travels invisibly across the atmosphere of a connected world.
1主语No single instrument of policy核心主语是 instrument，no single 加强语气，译作“任何单一的政策手段都不……”
2让步插入语however elegant相当于 no matter how elegant it is，省略了主语与 be 动词，作让步状语
3谓语部分could confine the technology within national bordersconfine sth within... 把……限制在……之内；情态动词 could 表“能够”
4原因状语从句because its most important component—mathematical knowledge—remains relevant and travels invisibly across the atmosphere of a connected world从句含两个并列谓语 remains relevant 与 travels invisibly；破折号之间是 component 的同位语
  • “however + 形容词/副词”相当于 no matter how...，引导让步状语，常以插入语形式出现并省略主谓，翻译时可前置为“无论多么……”
  • because 引导原因状语从句，从句中 and 并列 remains 与 travels 两个谓语，共享主语 component，阅读时要防止把第二个谓语误判为独立句子
  • 破折号之间的 mathematical knowledge 是同位语，对 component 作解释说明，翻译时可保留破折号或改写为“即……”
  • 翻译技巧：先处理让步插入语，再按“主句 + 因为从句”的汉语因果语序重组，长定语 of a connected world 宜译为独立的短句

🔷 长难句拆解｜not...but... 平行结构与两组并列的 as...as 比较结构

句子骨架：safety is not ... but ..., as constant ... as checking that ..., and as attentive to ... as to ...
Their conviction was simple: safety is not a marketing slogan but a discipline, as constant and unglamorous as checking that each integer in a dosage table is correct before a nurse administers a dose, and as attentive to every wrinkle in the protocol as to the protocol itself.
1主句Their conviction was simple冒号之后的内容是对 simple 的具体说明，起同位解释作用
2并列表语safety is not a marketing slogan but a disciplinenot...but... 连接两个对等名词短语，意为“不是……而是……”
3比较结构一as constant and unglamorous as checking that each integer in a dosage table is correct before a nurse administers a doseas...as 之间是两个并列形容词；checking 为动名词，后接 that 宾语从句，before 引导时间状语从句
4比较结构二as attentive to every wrinkle in the protocol as to the protocol itself与上一组 as...as 平行，第二个 as 之后省略了与上文相同的 attentive
  • not A but B 是平行结构，要求 A、B 语法成分对等，语气上先否定后肯定，译文可用“不是……而是……”保持对比
  • and 并列两个 as...as... 结构，共同作后置定语修饰 a discipline；第二个结构中 as to the protocol itself 是省略形式，相当于 as attentive to the protocol itself
  • checking that... 中 that 引导宾语从句；before 引导时间状语从句交代动作先后
  • 翻译技巧：英语的多重后置比较定语不宜直译成一长串“的”，应先译主干“安全是一种纪律”，再用“像……一样；对……也同样……”分层展开

🔷 长难句拆解｜nor 位于分句句首引起的部分倒装

句子骨架：... few denied that ...: people no longer treated ..., nor did they dismiss it as ...
The approximate effect of all this was hard to quantify, but few denied that something fundamental had shifted: people no longer treated each new tool as destiny, nor did they dismiss it as a mere companion to be ignored.
1并列分句一The approximate effect of all this was hard to quantifybe hard to do 中不定式用主动形式表示被动含义，即“难以被量化”
2并列分句二but few denied that something fundamental had shiftedthat 引导 denied 的宾语从句；few 形式肯定、意义否定，意为“几乎没有”
3同位解释句people no longer treated each new tool as destiny冒号后解释 shifted 的具体内容；treat sth as sth 把……当作……
4倒装分句nor did they dismiss it as a mere companion to be ignored否定词 nor 位于句首引起部分倒装，助动词 did 提至主语之前；to be ignored 作后置定语修饰 companion
  • nor/neither/never/seldom 等否定意义的词置于分句句首时，句子要部分倒装：nor + 助动词/情态动词 + 主语 + 动词原形
  • deny 后接 that 宾语从句；few denied 是隐含否定，译作“几乎没有人否认”，注意不要把意思译反
  • 不定式 to be ignored 作 companion 的后置定语且含被动意味，可译为“可以被忽视的、无足轻重的”
  • 翻译技巧：倒装句翻译时先还原为正常语序 they did not dismiss it either，再按汉语习惯译为“也不把它当作……”，无需保留倒装形式
