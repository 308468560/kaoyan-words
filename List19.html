🔷 长难句拆解｜where 引导非限制性定语从句，插入句中造成主谓分隔

句子骨架：In the high desert of Arizona, [where 非限制性定语从句], a narrow road follows a shallow river across the open prairie.
In the high desert of Arizona, where a scattered line of homesteads clings to the rim of a red sandstone ridge, a narrow road follows a shallow river across the open prairie.
1地点状语In the high desert of Arizona介词短语置于句首交代地理背景，全句真正主干被推迟出现。
2非限制性定语从句where a scattered line of homesteads clings to the rim of a red sandstone ridgewhere 相当于 in which，修饰先行词 desert，作补充说明；插在状语与主句之间，使主谓分隔。
3主语a narrow road主句主语在定语从句结束后才出现，是全句真正的陈述对象。
4谓语follows主句谓语，一般现在时与全文叙事基调保持一致。
5宾语与地点状语a shallow river across the open prairiea shallow river 作 follows 的宾语，across 介词短语补充方向。
  • where 在此引导非限制性定语从句，相当于 in which，只对先行词 desert 补充信息，去掉后主句仍然完整。
  • 长定语从句插在句首状语与主句主语之间，造成主谓分隔；阅读时应先跳过插入成分，抓住主干 a narrow road follows a shallow river。
  • cling to 本意为‘紧贴、附着’，此处拟人化描写农舍沿山脊分布；scattered 是过去分词作前置定语，意为‘散落的’。

🔷 长难句拆解｜三组谓语平行排比＋even when 引导的让步状语从句

句子骨架：She refused to..., refused to..., and treated ... as ..., even when ... threatened to arm ... and pull ... down.
She refused to drag her grief through every conversation, refused to spit bitterness at the healthy, and treated self-pity as a silly luxury, even when a sudden rip in the clinic's budget threatened to arm every pessimist with fresh evidence and pull the whole household down.
1主语She指代上文中的 Maria，一个主语贯穿后面三个并列谓语。
2并列谓语一refused to drag her grief through every conversationrefuse to do sth.；drag one's grief through... 是比喻，指让悲伤蔓延到每次谈话中。
3并列谓语二refused to spit bitterness at the healthy重复 refused to 构成排比；the healthy 为‘the＋形容词’，指代健康的人这一类群体。
4并列谓语三treated self-pity as a silly luxurytreat A as B 意为‘把 A 当作 B’，收束三项并列动作。
5让步状语从句even when a sudden rip in the clinic's budget threatened to arm every pessimist with fresh evidence and pull the whole household downeven when 引导让步从句；从句中 threatened 后接 arm... 与 pull... 两个并列不定式，pull 前省略 to。
  • 主干为 She refused to..., refused to..., and treated...，三个谓语平行并列，重复 refused to 增强语势，翻译时应保留排比节奏。
  • ‘the＋形容词’可表示一类人，如 the healthy（健康者）；even when 比单用 when 让步语气更强，意为‘即便在……的时候’。
  • threaten to do sth. 中 arm sb. with sth.（用某物武装某人）和 pull the whole household down（拖垮全家）共用一个不定式符号 to，属于不定式的省略并列。

🔷 长难句拆解｜that 宾语从句后接三个过去分词短语排比，内嵌省略关系代词的定语从句

句子骨架：The town had learned that renewal is a discipline: rooted in..., aimed at..., and measured by the lives (that) it allows sb. to live...
The town had simply learned, together, that renewal is a discipline: rooted in memory, aimed at evidence, and measured by the lives it allows ordinary people to live with dignity.
1主句The town had simply learned过去完成时 had learned 表示到那时为止已经懂得；together 在原文中作插入副词。
2宾语从句that renewal is a disciplinethat 引导 learned 的宾语从句，从句用一般现在时，表明这是一条普遍道理。
3分词状语一rooted in memory过去分词短语，逻辑主语是 renewal，相当于 which is rooted in memory。
4分词状语二aimed at evidence与 rooted、measured 构成三项排比，从三个方面解释 discipline。
5分词状语三（含定语从句）measured by the lives it allows ordinary people to live with dignityit allows ordinary people to live with dignity 是省略 that/which 的定语从句，修饰 the lives；allow sb. to do sth. 表示‘使某人能够做某事’。
  • 冒号后 rooted in..., aimed at..., and measured by... 三个过去分词短语平行并列，共同解释 renewal is a discipline，是典型的分词状语排比。
  • the lives it allows ordinary people to live 中，lives 既是 measured by 的宾语，又是定语从句中 live 的逻辑宾语；关系代词 that/which 在从句中作宾语，故省略。
  • 句末 with dignity 是介词短语作方式状语修饰 live；allow 此处不是‘允许’，而是‘使……得以（做成）’之意。
