句子骨架：主句 She warned me + that 宾语从句，其后 because 引导原因状语从句
She warned me that one wrong number could embarrass us nationwide, because our stories now travel far beyond the county line.
1主句She warned mewarn sb that... 结构中，me 是宾语，that 从句说明警告的内容

🔷 长难句拆解｜两个并列的 that 宾语从句 + not merely 递进否定

句子骨架：主句 He believes + that 从句一 + and that 从句二，构成并列宾语
He believes that education should make people stronger, not merely more employable, and that knowledge without sympathy is a dangerous tool.
1主句He believes主语加谓语，后接两个并列的 that 从句作宾语
2宾语从句一that education should make people stronger, not merely more employablemake + 宾语 + 形容词作宾补；not merely 表示“不仅仅是”，与前面的比较级形成递进
3宾语从句二and that knowledge without sympathy is a dangerous tool第二个 that 不能省略，与第一个从句并列；without sympathy 作后置定语
  • 两个 that 从句由 and 并列，第二个 that 必须保留，否则读者会误以为并列的是句子成分。
  • make people stronger 是 make + 宾语 + 形容词的复合宾语结构。
  • not merely 与 more employable 形成对比，写作中常用来表达价值判断。

🔷 长难句拆解｜enough to do 结果结构 + which 非限制性定语从句 + 比较级

句子骨架：主句 The factory is large enough to employ half the town + which 引导非限制性定语从句
The factory is large enough to employ half the town, which makes the case harder than it looks; necessity, one worker told me, is a poor excuse for a lie, and regrettably the fine was small and the jobs stayed.
1主句The factory is large enough to employ half the town形容词 + enough to do 表示“足够……以至于能做某事”
2定语从句which makes the case harder than it lookswhich 指代前面整句内容，makes 用第三人称单数；harder than 构成比较结构
  • enough 修饰形容词时必须后置：large enough，不能说 enough large。
  • 非限制性定语从句的 which 可指代整个主句，这里说明“工厂雇了半镇人”带来的后果。
  • make the case harder 中 harder 是宾语补足语，than it looks 是比较状语从句的省略形式。
