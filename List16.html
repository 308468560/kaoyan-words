🔷 长难句拆解｜like 介词短语 + enough to 结构

句子骨架：主句 A rock formation enclosed the village + like 短语 + and 并列分句，含 enough ... to ... 结构
A rock formation enclosed the village like a pair of hands, and the air was thin enough at this altitude to make me stop and breathe.
1主句A rock formation enclosed the village like a pair of handslike a pair of hands 为介词短语作方式状语，说明围住的方式
2并列分句and the air was thin enough at this altitudeenough 修饰形容词 thin，置于其后；at this altitude 为地点状语
3结果不定式to make me stop and breatheenough to do 结构，不定式表示结果；stop 与 breathe 并列
  • like a pair of hands 是比喻性的方式状语，不是定语，阅读时不要误接到 village 上。
  • enough 修饰形容词时必须后置：thin enough，不能写成 enough thin。
  • make sb do sth 中 stop and breathe 是省略 to 的并列不定式。

🔷 长难句拆解｜and 并列分句 + that 定语从句

句子骨架：主句 The side ... was ours + and 分句 + that 引导定语从句修饰 document
The side of the road he wanted was ours, and thus the case turned on a single document that a thief could have taken in a second.
1主句The side of the road he wanted was ourshe wanted 是省略关系代词的定语从句，先行词为 the side
2并列分句and thus the case turned on a single documentthus 为连接副词，turn on 意为“取决于”
3定语从句that a thief could have taken in a secondthat 引导定语从句修饰 document；could have taken 表示对过去能力的推测
  • The side of the road he wanted 中，he wanted 前省略了 that/which，先行词是 the side。
  • turn on 在此不是“打开”，而是“取决于”，是考研阅读中的常见多义短语。
  • could have taken 是情态动词 + have done，表示“本来能够做到”，隐含“却没有”的意味。
