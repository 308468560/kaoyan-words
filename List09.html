句子骨架：主句 Her technique was old-fashioned but precise + and she insisted that 从句
Her technique was old-fashioned but precise, and she insisted that we learn the technic of asking small questions.
1主句Her technique was old-fashioned but precisebut 连接两个作表语的形容词，构成并列
2并列谓语and she insistedand 连接第二个分句，主语 she 承前，谓语用一般过去时
3宾语从句that we learn the technic of asking small questionsinsist 后的 that 从句用动词原形 learn，体现虚拟语气；of asking 作后置定语
  • insist 表示“坚持要求”时，that 从句用 (should) + 动词原形，learn 即为原形。
  • old-fashioned but precise 是两个形容词并列作表语，but 表转折。
  • of asking small questions 是介词短语作定语，修饰 the technic。

🔷 长难句拆解｜who 定语从句 + 省略 that 的宾语从句 + and 并列分句

句子骨架：主句 I sat alongside a young mother + who 定语从句（内含 said 的宾语从句）+ and I wrote that down
I sat alongside a young mother who said the whole argument needed a punch of honesty, and I wrote that down.
1主句I sat alongside a young motheralongside 是介词，后接名词作宾语
2定语从句who said the whole argument needed a punch of honestywho 指代 a young mother，在从句中作主语；said 后为省略 that 的宾语从句
3并列分句and I wrote that downand 连接并列谓语，that 指代前面整句话的内容
  • who 引导的定语从句修饰人，先行词是 a young mother。
  • said 后的宾语从句省略了 that，从句主语 the whole argument，谓语 needed。
  • that 在此为指示代词，回指母亲说的那句话，而非引导从句。

🔷 长难句拆解｜must 表义务 + or 引出的并列结果分句

句子骨架：主句 She said + 宾语从句（every generation must strive...）+ or 引出另一个并列分句
She said every generation must strive to explain science in its own language, or the explanation will wear thin and become a slogan.
1主句She saidsaid 后接宾语从句，说明她说话的内容
2宾语从句every generation must strive to explain science in its own languagemust 表义务；strive to do sth 表示“努力做某事”
3并列结果or the explanation will wear thin and become a sloganor 表示“否则”，连接一个独立分句；will wear and become 为并列谓语
  • or 在此不是“或者”而是“否则”，前面是条件，后面是否则会发生的后果。
  • strive to do sth 是高频搭配，比 try 更强调付出努力。
  • wear thin 是固定表达，意为“逐渐失去力量、变得空洞”。
