🔷 长难句拆解｜so ... that 结构引导的结果状语从句

句子骨架：主句 a turbulent storm broke over the valley + and 并列分句，其中 so intense that ... 表结果
In July a turbulent storm broke over the valley, and the rain was so intense that the road became a stream.
1并列分句一In July a turbulent storm broke over the valleyIn July 为时间状语；broke 为 break 的过去式，表示“爆发”
2并列分句二and the rain was so intense that the road became a streamso + 形容词 + that 从句，表示“如此……以至于”，that 从句是结果状语从句
  • so ... that ... 中间必须接形容词或副词，不能直接接名词。
  • that 引导的结果状语从句本身结构完整，road 作从句主语。
  • and 连接两个并列分句，叙事时常用这种结构推进画面。

🔷 长难句拆解｜两个 that 宾语从句并列 + and 连接

句子骨架：主句 Our lawyer argued + that 从句一，and that 从句二
Our lawyer argued that the federal constitution protected the village's water, and that the jurisdiction belonged to the provincial court.
1主句Our lawyer arguedargue 表示“主张、辩称”，后面常接 that 从句
2宾语从句一that the federal constitution protected the village's waterthat 引导宾语从句，主语是 the federal constitution
3宾语从句二and that the jurisdiction belonged to the provincial court第二个 that 不能省略，与第一个 that 从句并列作 argued 的宾语
  • 两个 that 从句并列时，第二个 that 通常保留，以免读者误读。
