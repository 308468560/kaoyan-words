🔷 长难句拆解｜范围状语 + 并列谓语 + 现在分词伴随状语

句子骨架：From…to…（范围状语）, 主语 + has had to do A and do B（并列谓语）, doing…（现在分词作伴随状语）.
From the earliest republic of the ancient world to our global civilization, each generation has had to examine its assumptions and take one instructive example from history, drawing ample lessons about both climate upheaval and human resilience.
1范围状语From the earliest republic of the ancient world to our global civilization介词短语 from…to… 横跨句首，交代时间跨度，意为“从……到……”。
2主语each generationeach 修饰单数 generation，谓语相应使用单数形式 has had to。
3并列谓语has had to examine its assumptions and take one instructive example from historyhas had to（现在完成时＋have to）后接两个不带 to 的并列动词 examine 与 take。
4伴随状语drawing ample lessons about both climate upheaval and human resilience现在分词短语表伴随动作；内含 both…and… 平行结构，lessons 为复数，呼应两方面教训。
  • from…to… 介词短语作范围状语，常置于句首统领全句。
  • have to 的现在完成时为 has/have had to，表示“到目前为止一直不得不”。
  • 情态结构后两个动词 examine 与 take 由 and 并列，构成平行谓语。
  • 现在分词 drawing 引导伴随状语；both A and B 要求 A、B 语法形式对称。

句子骨架：Citizens, who…, who pick facts… and regard A—插入举例—as B, will distrust 宾语 + who 定语从句.
Citizens who exercise their judgment, who pick facts with care and regard every finite resource—even a slice of bread or a lump of coal—as shared inheritance, will distrust the professional troublemaker who feeds on panic.
1主语Citizens全句主语，后续两个 who 从句均对其进行非限制性补充说明。
2定语从句一who exercise their judgment第一个 who 从句，描述公民的第一项品质：运用判断力。
3定语从句二who pick facts with care and regard every finite resource—even a slice of bread or a lump of coal—as shared inheritance第二个 who 从句含并列谓语 pick 与 regard；regard A as B 之间被破折号插入语 even…coal… 举例分隔。
4谓语与宾语will distrust the professional troublemaker who feeds on panicwill distrust 是全句谓语（一般将来时）；宾语 troublemaker 后再带一个 who 限制性定语从句。
  • 两个 who 并列引导非限制性定语从句，共同修饰 Citizens，形成多重后置定语。
  • regard A as B 意为“把 A 视为 B”，A 与 as 之间被破折号插入语分隔，阅读时需跨位找搭配。
  • 主句谓语 will distrust 与主语 Citizens 被两个定语从句远远隔开，属主谓分隔现象。
  • 句末 who feeds on panic 为限制性定语从句，修饰 the professional troublemaker。
