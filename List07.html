🔷 长难句拆解｜who 引导的定语从句 + and 并列宾语

句子骨架：主句 The children preferred the one about a king + who 定语从句 + and 并列第二个宾语
The children preferred the one about a king who buried his relic under a volcano, and a story about a heaven made of bread.
1主句The children preferred the one about a kingthe one 指代前面提到的某个故事，about a king 后置修饰 the one
2定语从句who buried his relic under a volcanowho 指代 a king，在从句中作主语；buried 为一般过去时
3并列宾语and a story about a heaven made of breadand 连接与 the one 并列的第二个宾语；made of bread 为过去分词短语作定语
  • the one 是代词，后面跟 about 短语说明它指的是哪一个。
  • who 引导的定语从句紧跟先行词 a king，不能换成 which。
  • made of bread 是过去分词短语作后置定语，表示“由面包做成的”。

句子骨架：It was unacceptable that ... + and the accounts had a gaping hole
It was unacceptable that a danger of this size sat on the edge of the square, and the accounts had a gaping hole.
1形式主语 + 表语It was unacceptableIt 为形式主语，真正的主语是后面的 that 从句
2主语从句that a danger of this size sat on the edge of the squarethat 引导主语从句，sat 为谓语，of this size 后置修饰 danger
3并列分句and the accounts had a gaping holeand 连接另一个独立分句，补充说明账目的问题
  • that 主语从句放在句首太长时，常用 it 作形式主语后置。
  • of this size 是后置定语，翻译时要提前：“这么大的”。
  • gaping hole 原指“张开的洞”，这里比喻账目上的巨大亏空。
